MAGINHAWA

Maginhawa is a footwear brand that bridges the gap between casual comfort and elevated style, offering thoughtfully designed men's footwear for the modern, fashion-conscious consumer. Inspired by the growing appeal of elevated casual fashion, the brand aims to redefine everyday footwear by combining the convenience of slip-on designs with the sophistication of traditional leather shoes.

Beyond style and functionality, Maginhawa takes pride in its commitment to local craftsmanship. By collaborating with skilled Marikina artisans and Marikina leather suppliers, the brand seeks to showcase the quality and authenticity of locally made footwear while supporting the communities and traditions behind the country's shoemaking industry.

Guided by the values of Chic, Classy, and Comfortable, Maginhawa envisions making sophisticated everyday footwear more accessible to a new generation of consumers. Through its emphasis on versatility, quality materials, and Filipino craftsmanship, the brand aims to demonstrate that locally made footwear can deliver both contemporary style and lasting comfort.

Maginhawa takes pride in supporting local businesses and preserving the shoemaking heritage of Marikina. Our footwear will be manufactured in collaboration with Risqué Manufacturing, a Filipino footwear manufacturer that works with local craftsmen and artisans. Through its small-batch manufacturing services, we aim to turn our designs into quality finished products while maintaining attention to detail, proper construction, and the overall comfort of each pair. This partnership allows us to focus on developing our brand and designs while working with experienced shoemakers who understand the process of footwear production.

For our leather and other material requirements, we plan to source from OTTO Leather, our identified leather supplier. The partnership will allow us to obtain the leather needed for our footwear designs, with material selection based on the desired texture, quality, color, and overall appearance of each collection. By working with a dedicated material supplier and a local manufacturing partner, Maginhawa aims to maintain consistency in its products while supporting businesses within the local footwear industry.

Through these collaborations, we bring together carefully selected materials and Filipino craftsmanship to create footwear that reflects our values of being Chic, Classy, and Comfortable. More than producing stylish everyday shoes, Maginhawa aims to contribute to the local shoemaking industry by working with Filipino suppliers, manufacturers, and skilled artisans.

Maginhawa stands out with a unique fusion of authentic Marikina craftsmanship, elevated casual aesthetics, and everyday practicality. Rather than offering only traditional leather footwear, the brand reimagines classic designs in modern slip-on silhouettes that blend sophistication with comfort. This approach lets customers enjoy the elegant look of leather shoes without sacrificing the convenience essential for daily wear.

Moreover, Maginhawa offers a cohesive collection that suits a range of preferences and occasions, from the contemporary Maginhawa Leather Mule to the more refined Penny Mule and the practical Daily Leather. Its limited-edition releases also showcase distinctive designs, providing customers with exclusive styling options.

Its competitive advantage arises not from a single product feature, but from the harmonious blend of local craftsmanship, contemporary design, and effortless versatility that defines the brand. This unique identity, supported by strong relationships with local artisans and a consistent design ethos, enables Maginhawa to create a brand experience competitors may find hard to replicate.

Revenue Model

Maginhawa will mainly generate revenue by selling its locally crafted leather footwear. The brand will follow a direct-to-consumer (D2C) business model, in which customers can purchase products through online platforms and social media channels. This allows the business to reach its target market, particularly Gen Z men and young professionals, while also reducing the additional costs from having intermediaries.

To further increase sales, the organization will utilize social media platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok to showcase its products, promote new releases, and reach potential customers. Digital lookbooks and promotional campaigns will also be used to present the brand's collections and encourage purchases. Additionally, the business may participate in pop-up markets and collaborate with selected local retailers to expand its market reach and provide customers with more opportunities to experience and purchase its products.

In terms of pricing, the company will consider its production costs, including genuine leather, materials, and labor from Marikina artisans, while maintaining prices that reflect the quality and craftsmanship of its products. The limited-edition releases will have a higher price point compared to the regular collections to account for their distinct materials and exclusivity.

Overall, Maginhawa's revenue model will focus on generating consistent income through its regular footwear collections while creating additional revenue opportunities through limited-edition releases and expanded sales channels.
